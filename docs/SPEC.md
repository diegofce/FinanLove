# SPEC.md

## Project: FinanLove

### Mission
A private financial management platform for Diego and his partner, designed for personal use, learning, and portfolio.

### Core Users
1. **Diego:** Admin/User.
2. **Partner:** User.

### Functional Modules (Planned)

#### 1. Authentication & Security
- [ ] Secure Login/Logout.
- [ ] User Profile management.
- [ ] Role-based access control (Admin/User).

#### 2. Dashboard
- [ ] Summary of total balance.
- [ ] Recent movements.
- [ ] Quick actions.

#### 3. Financial Management
- [ ] **Accounts:** Bank accounts, cash, digital wallets.
- [ ] **Income:** Recurring and one-time.
- [ ] **Expenses:** Categorized spending.
- [ ] **Transactions:** Moving money between accounts.

#### 4. Shared Finance (Social Features)
- [ ] **Debts:** Tracking who owes whom.
- [ ] **Loans:** Requesting and accepting loans between the two users.
- [ ] **Shared Expenses:** Splitting bills.

#### 5. Planning
- [ ] **Budgets:** Monthly limits per category.
- [ ] **Goals:** Saving for specific targets (trips, purchases).

#### 6. Notifications
- [ ] Low balance alerts.
- [ ] Budget exceeded alerts.
- [ ] Loan requests.

### Technical Constraints
- No `any` in TypeScript.
- Strict typing in Python.
- 100% test coverage for business logic.
- Documented ADRs for every major decision.
